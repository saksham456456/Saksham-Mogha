## Idea

A playful Android app that serves witty, relatable excuses for everyday situations, from a strict boss to "tech issues".

## Key decisions

- **Build-time content generation**: a Python script produces 1000+ excuses in nine categories and bundles them as local JSON, so the app works fully **offline**.
- **Personalisation**: the app learns your name and persona during onboarding.
- **Kotlin + Room + Coroutines** for a product-quality local-first architecture.

## Stack

Kotlin · Room · Coroutines · Python (build-time script)
