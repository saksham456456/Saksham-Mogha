## Problem

Top-10 lists are subjective, and rating ten things at once is hard. Choosing between two is easy.

## Approach

Tiebreak shows binary matchups ("React vs Svelte") and feeds each vote into an **Elo rating system** that uses rating deviation and Wilson lower bounds. Over time the community's votes add up to a stable tier list.

## Key decisions

- **Redis-backed streams (Upstash)** absorb concurrent votes so Postgres isn't locked on every swipe.
- **Supabase Auth with anonymous identities** so people can vote right away and merge into an account later.
- **Swipeable 3D cards** make voting fast and tactile.

## Stack
