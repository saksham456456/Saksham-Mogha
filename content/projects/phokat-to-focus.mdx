## Problem

Students lose hours to distraction. Most focus apps just run a timer.

## Approach

Phokat-To-Focus combines planning, coaching and enforcement:

- **AI planner** builds study schedules from your goals and deadlines.
- **AI coach** to chat with when you are stuck or need motivation.
- **Strict mode (Android)** keeps you in the app during a focus session using system-level overlays.
- **Progress tracking** with charts of study habits over time.

## Stack

Flutter · Provider · Firebase (Auth, Firestore) · Google Gemini · Android and web
