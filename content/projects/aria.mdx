## Problem

In online classes, teachers often talk to a wall of muted microphones. Students who are stuck tend to stay silent, and the teacher never finds out.

## Approach

ARIA joins the video call as a participant. Built on the **Agora Conversational AI Engine**, she helps explain difficult topics with a Socratic style, nudges struggling students, runs pop quizzes and points out learning gaps, all in real time.

## Context

ARIA was **built for the EchoSphere Hackathon**. The repository links to a project overview and a short demo video.

## Stack

TypeScript · Agora Conversational AI · real-time video and audio rooms
