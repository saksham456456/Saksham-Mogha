## Idea

One assistant, several modes: general chat, a coding-focused persona and live voice conversation.

## Key decisions

- **Client-side persistence with IndexedDB**, so conversations survive reloads and stay usable offline.
- **Google Gemini** as the model provider.

## Stack

React · TypeScript · Google Gemini · IndexedDB
