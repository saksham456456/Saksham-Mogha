## Problem

Most AI chat apps send every prompt to a remote API. That costs money per request and means your conversation leaves your machine.

## Approach

Saksham-AI loads a quantized model into the browser and runs inference locally on the GPU through **WebLLM** and **WebGPU**. Once the model is cached, there is no API key to manage and no per-request server cost, and prompts stay on the device.

## Key decisions

- **On-device inference over a hosted API**, trading a one-time model download for privacy and zero running cost.
- **Next.js + TypeScript** for the app shell, with **Zustand** for lightweight client state.

## Stack

Next.js · TypeScript · WebLLM · WebGPU · Zustand
