## Idea

Drift is an anonymous **thought network**, not a chat app. You release short anonymous thoughts ("drifts") into a dark ambient ocean and catch the ones that speak to you.

## Notable design choices

- **No friend lists.** If a conversation changes you, you can form a one-time "constellation" beacon to find each other once more.
- **Privacy first**: anonymity is the core of the product, not a setting.

## Stack

100% Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Clean Architecture (domain → data) · Firebase
