## Problem

Staying informed takes too long when every story is a long read.

## Approach

Breefo's **Smart Summary Engine** pulls the most important information out of each article and shows a TL;DR plus key points. You can switch between seven categories: general, business, technology, science, health, entertainment and sports.

## Stack

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Material 3 · MVVM
